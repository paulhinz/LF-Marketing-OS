## 4. Positioning Statement

For enterprises and engineers building the next generation of AI and cloud native-powered systems, CNCF is the home of open technologies that enable observability, inference, orchestration, and agentic systems to run at global scale — open, portable, and sovereign.

## 5. Messaging Pillars

### Pillar 1: Cloud Native Is the Railway — AI Is the Train
CNCF projects are the modern railways of AI computing. As "trains" evolve (models, agents) the tracks must remain stable, interoperable and global reaching. Kubernetes is no longer just a container orchestrator — with AI-specific APIs, conformance programs, GPU and resource management upgrades, and model-serving frameworks, it is evolving into the control plane for AI infrastructure. CNCF projects already power production inference, workload scheduling, and ML orchestration.
Example: Kubernetes, AI Conformance Program, PARK Stack

### Pillar 2: Observability: The Control Loop for AI Systems
Modern observability is more than troubleshooting — it's becoming an intelligence layer solving problems like runaway costs, data overload, and the need for smarter sampling and analysis of massively distributed systems. The CNCF provides the governance, conformance, and ecosystem maturity that lets observability evolve from monitoring systems to powering automated and predictive operations.
Example: OpenTelemetry Graduation, Fluent, Prometheus, Users (Oracle, OpenSearch, Case Study submissions)

### Pillar 3: The Future of AI Is Realized Through Cloud Native Systems
The CNCF is laying the foundation for dynamic orchestration — where cloud-native systems become the operational layer for AI, agents, and next-generation applications, running thousands of specialized models, retraining weekly or monthly, and routing dynamically based on task, cost, latency, and policy. In 2025, more than 40 projects joined the CNCF.
Example: Michelangelo, HAMi project

### Pillar 4: CNCF Enables Digital Sovereignty
Infrastructure independence is crucial for AI sovereignty. CNCF projects are portable, customizable, transparent and governable. The CNCF is ahead of data and privacy demands through its governance, support for and creation of open standards, and building conformance programs to enable regulatory compliance and digital sovereignty.
Example: AI Conformance, OTel, CNCF proper (KubeCon India and EU particularly)

### Pillar 5: Open Source Community in the AI Era
Technical roles are evolving to meet the increasing demands of always-on AI workloads. The CNCF is where the community comes together to learn, network, and make decisions that shape the future of technology the world depends on.
Example: Ambassadors, Maintainers, Users, Community/Enthusiasts
