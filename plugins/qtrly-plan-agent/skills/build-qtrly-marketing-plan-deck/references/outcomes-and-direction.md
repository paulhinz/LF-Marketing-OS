# Business Outcomes and Direction — Phase 2 reference

## The six business outcomes

Per the LFX Marketing OS planning model, every goal aligns to exactly one:

| Outcome | What it means | Typical KPI shapes |
|---|---|---|
| Event attendance | Fill the seats at project events | registrations vs. capacity, reg pace/week |
| Event sponsorship | Sell event sponsorships (revenue) | $ sold vs. target, # sponsors by tier |
| Education | Course enrollments and certifications (revenue) | enrollments, cert completions, course revenue |
| Membership | New members and renewals (revenue); secure commitment via targeted lists | new members by tier, renewal rate, $ ARR |
| Awareness | Grow the audience that all other outcomes draw from | audience size (social + newsletter + web), share of voice |
| Adoption | Grow usage of the project itself; build advocacy | downloads/usage proxy, contributor growth, community engagement |

Revenue outcomes: event attendance, event sponsorship, education, membership. Community outcomes: awareness, adoption. Awareness "drives potential across all" — a deemphasized awareness outcome still needs a maintenance floor.

## Direction semantics (per outcome)

- **Increase focus** → increased funding; expect at least one goal against it
- **Hold** → funding to be determined/flat; goal optional (maintenance KPI)
- **Deemphasize** → lowered funding; no goal, or an explicit maintenance floor stated in the plan so the reduction is a decision, not neglect

## Stage-based defaults (propose, then confirm)

| Project stage | Recommended emphasis |
|---|---|
| Has product & community | Membership growth, events, education (monetize a healthy base) |
| Has product, no community | Community/adoption: awareness via content marketing, ambassador enablement |
| No product, no community | Awareness: influential memberships and project adoption |

Adjust defaults with Phase 1 signals before presenting, e.g.: event reg pace far below capacity → raise event attendance; renewals at risk → raise membership; contributor decline → raise adoption/community; audience flat while market grows → raise awareness.

## Budget rules

- Total quarterly budget comes from the ED/PL; never infer it.
- Reserve a **discretionary budget** for opportunistic execution (unforeseen market needs, viral moments, enthusiastic new members). Suggest 10–15%. Discretionary spend must stay within the reserve and be reported in the next QBR.
- Named goal budgets + discretionary reserve = total. Show the roll-up whenever budgets change.

## Constraints to capture

- Fixed commitments: contracted events, sponsorship obligations already signed
- LF BU services in negotiation (Events, Training, LF Media, Creative/Web, Membership Sales) — these shape what goals are executable
- Team capacity: who actually does the work this quarter
