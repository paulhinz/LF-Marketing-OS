# Quality Rubric — score before delivery (all must pass)

## Process integrity
1. Every phase was confirmed by the user — no unconfirmed suggestions made it into the deck.
2. Goal count matches the user's choice; goals are ranked and the ranking was confirmed.

## Goal quality
3. Every goal has all six attributes: rank/name, one business-outcome alignment, KPI with baseline, budget, timeline, risks with mitigations.
4. Every KPI is a number measurable against a named source (LFX, document, or manual owner).
5. No goal is a disguised campaign — no channel, tactic, or content-calendar language at goal level.
6. Every "increase focus" outcome has at least one goal; every "deemphasize" outcome has either no goal or an explicit maintenance floor.

## Data integrity
7. No invented figures: each number traces to LFX, a provided document, or a user-supplied "(manual)" value recorded in speaker notes.
8. Goal budgets + discretionary reserve sum exactly to the confirmed total budget.
9. Review slide (or Baseline slide, for initial plans) reflects user corrections from the confirmation step.

## Deck quality
10. Slide count within outline bounds (~10–14 for 3–5 goals; scale goal slides only).
11. Layout follows references/deck-outline.md; Brand Kit styling applied when available; body text ≥ 18pt.
12. Speaker notes carry sources and rationale for every goal slide.
13. Decisions & next steps slide names the CFT hand-off and the next quarterly review date.

## Scoring
Pass/fail per criterion. On any failure, revise and re-score. Maximum 3 revision cycles; then present the best draft with a plain list of still-failing criteria.
