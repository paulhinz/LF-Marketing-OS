# Quality rubric

Score every criterion pass or fail before delivery. Revise on any fail, up to three cycles; then deliver the best draft with the failing criteria listed at the top of the open items.

## Inputs and provenance

1. All three foundation documents were read, with file, version, and date named on the cover; a missing one is named and its agent recommended.
2. Every connector read (Octolens, LFX Lens, LFX, HubSpot, Segment, Drive) is listed in Appendix A with tool, query, date, and any warning or refusal verbatim.
3. No read changed anything: no keywords, feeds, lists, segments, committees, files, or messages were created, modified, sent, or deleted.

## Section 1: Earned media

4. Every outlet belongs to one of the five slide-12 tiers and each priority group is labelled with its tier number.
5. The priority order is explained in one paragraph per group and follows the ICP's business outcomes in priority order.
6. Every outlet row has fit (persona), strategy, and a lead message traceable to a Message Foundation pillar, value message, sound bite, or objection stance, with Stat Bank IDs for every figure.
7. No reporter is named without a source in hand; no competitor is criticized; no pricing is quoted; no superlative appears without proof in the same sentence.
8. News hooks are listed with timing, tiers served, and Stat Bank IDs; Tier 1 is marked "pitch only with a hook".

## Section 2: Social voices

9. Between 5 and 15 voices are ranked, each with handle and platform, reach and activity (with window), why they matter (persona or pillar), tier (A, B, or C), and next action with a CTA ID.
10. Ranking factors and exclusions are stated; excluded promotional accounts are listed in Appendix B; official, member, media, and bot accounts are separated into a reference list.
11. Scale metrics (mentions, authors, platforms, sentiment, months) carry the window and the source.
12. Support and incentives fit the Brand Kit; no paid coverage; channel rules stated.
13. Keyword gaps and handle decisions are recorded as open items.

## Section 3: Existing audiences

14. Fewer than 20 audiences are recommended; each has owner foundation, size and system, overlap score and persona, overlapping message with CTA ID, and the route to use it.
15. Sizes are the connector's figures with their read date; "unknown" where not visible; no sums across event pools.
16. The HubSpot portal identity is confirmed or the gap is stated; Segment is named as a gap when no connector exists.
17. Governance of Groups.io lists, event pools, and member lists is stated in "How to use these lists".

## Document

18. Built on the LF Agent DOCS template through lf-output-formatter with the project's brand; cover logo, header, footer, and page numbers render; no leftover template tokens; tables fit portrait pages.
19. Brand Kit casing, member noun, and governance and trademark sentences are correct throughout; Appendix C carries them verbatim.
20. Plain style; every "(manual)" item is collected in an open items subsection; the executive summary names the three or four actions required before any list is used.
