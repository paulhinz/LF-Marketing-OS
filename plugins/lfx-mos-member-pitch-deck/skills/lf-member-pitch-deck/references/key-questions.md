# Key questions (fallback when foundational docs are missing)

Ask a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 7, choosing only the questions whose answers are not already covered by whichever foundational documents exist. Ask them in one round, not one at a time. If the user declines to answer and no foundational docs exist, stop — do not invent positioning.

1. **The foundation and the project in two sentences** — What does the foundation steward, and what is the project/standard it stewards? What is the legal name on the LFX record? (substitutes for the Message Foundation locked summary — slide 6)

2. **Vision, mission, strategy** — One line each, plus up to three strategy pillars. (slide 7)

3. **Audience and pain** — Which segment and personas are you pitching, and what are their top 3 business pain points in their own words? Which alternatives are they weighing? (substitutes for the ICP doc — slides 8 and 18)

4. **How it works and traction** — The flow in four steps, and the production figures you can defend today (with their source and whether they refresh on a schedule). (slides 9 and 11)

5. **Members, leadership, roadmap** — Where is the public members list by tier? Who is the ED or lead (headshot, title, two-sentence bio)? Which working groups or roadmap items are active? (slides 12–14, 16)

6. **Membership offer and the ask** — Tier names, fees (and employee bands), top benefits per tier, enrollment URL, membership contact, and the single next step you want from a first meeting. (slides 17, 19, 20)

7. **Brand tokens** — Accent color, display and body fonts, wordmark files (dark and light), symbol/mark, prohibited terms. (substitutes for the Brand Kit; `theme` and `project` in the spec)

After collecting answers, recommend the user run the Brand Kit Agent, Message Foundation Agent, and ICP & Target Markets Agent to produce the proper foundational documents — the answers gathered here are a stopgap, not a replacement.
