# Key questions (fallback when foundational docs are missing)

Ask a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 7, choosing only the questions whose answers are not already covered by whichever foundational documents exist. Ask them in one round, not one at a time. If the user declines to answer and no foundational docs exist, stop — do not invent positioning.

1. **The project in one sentence** — What does the project do, and why does it matter right now? (substitutes for the Message Foundation's locked summary — slide 4 and "why now" — slide 5)

2. **Audience** — Which segment and personas are you pitching, and what are their top 3 business pain points in their own words? (substitutes for the ICP doc — slides 2, 3, and 16)

3. **Traction proof** — What evidence exists that the project already works: contributors, releases, production deployments, notable adopters? (slide 10)

4. **Social proof** — Which approved case studies, member quotes, or member logos can be used? (slides 11–13; only pre-approved material)

5. **Membership offer** — What are the membership tiers, pricing, and headline benefits? (slides 15–16)

6. **The ask** — What tier do you recommend to this segment, and what is the single next step you want from the meeting? (slide 18)

7. **Brand constraints** — Any brand guidelines to follow: colors, logo files, tone, prohibited terms? (substitutes for the Brand Kit; deck styling and rubric brand checks)

After collecting answers, recommend the user run the Brand Kit Agent, Message Foundation Agent, and ICP & Target Markets Agent to produce the proper foundational documents — the answers gathered here are a stopgap, not a replacement.
