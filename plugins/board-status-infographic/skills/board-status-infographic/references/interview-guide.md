# Interview guide

Ask only what the sources did not answer. Group the questions into two or three short
batches so the ED can answer in a few minutes. Offer your best pre-filled answer with
each question when you have one from the plan deck, the pre-read, or LFX; it is faster
for the ED to correct than to compose.

## Batch 0: sources (always first)

1. "Do you have a link to a document with this meeting's goals and the issues the board
   needs to take up? A board pre-read, an agenda, last quarter's plan, or a memo all work."
2. "Is there a current Quarterly Marketing Plan deck for [project]? (I looked in [places]
   and found / did not find one.)"
3. "Which meeting is this for, and when? Who presents?" (feeds `header`)

If they give a document, read it fully before asking anything else. Most of batches 1
and 2 are usually answered by it.

## Batch 1: the frame

4. "In one sentence, what do you want the board to take away from this meeting?"
   (the `headline`; use their words)
5. "What are you asking the board for: money, votes, a direction, or just attention?
   How much, on what?" (the `ask`)
6. "Which of these fits this meeting best: strategy or annual plan, quarterly review,
   budget, governance or membership, or something else?" (chooses the block shape)

## Batch 2: the numbers

7. "Which four to six numbers show how far the foundation has come? Give me the starting
   point and today for each." (`scorecard`) Offer LFX reads for members, contributors,
   projects, events, and training if connected.
8. "Did you survey members or the community recently? What are the two or three figures
   that matter, and is there a split by tier, region, or segment worth showing?" (`stats`)
9. For a quarterly review: "For each goal in the plan, what is the KPI, the target, and
   where it stands today? Which are done, on track, at risk, off track?" (`goals`)
   Pre-fill from the plan deck and ask only for the "now" values and statuses.
10. "What is the single request or quote from members you want the board to remember?"
    (`stats.callout`)

## Batch 3: risks, plans, decisions

11. "What are the two to four risks the board can actually do something about? For each,
    what is the consequence and when does it bite?" (`risks`)
12. "Where is the money going next period? Name the areas, in priority order, and whether
    allocations are final." (`numbered`)
13. "What are the key dates between now and the next board meeting?" (`timeline`)
14. "List the items the board must act on, in agenda order. For each, is it a vote, a
    review ahead of a later vote, a discussion, or for information only? Where is the
    detail (exhibit, slide, document)?" (`decisions`)
15. "What sources should the footer credit, and as of what date?"

## Batch 4: finish

16. "Any Brand Kit or colors to use?" (default is fine if not)
17. "Letter or A4?"
18. "Anything on the page you would rather leave for the verbal update?"

## What to do with "I don't know"

Leave the block out or mark the value with "(TBC)" and tell the ED it is there. Never
invent a number. If a whole batch comes back empty, the meeting probably does not need
that block; the adaptive layout exists so the page can be shorter.
