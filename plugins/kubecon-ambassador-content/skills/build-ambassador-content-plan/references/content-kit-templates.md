# Content kit templates

Use these formats verbatim in the per-ambassador briefs. Every placeholder in [brackets] must be filled with real, personalized content — never ship a bracket to an ambassador.

## Video prompt (30–60 second selfie video)

```
VIDEO PROMPT — [Session title], [Day] [Time]
Where: film at [location suggestion: session room entrance / booth / hallway]
Hook (first 5 seconds): "[One-line hook tied to the ambassador's expertise]"
Middle (20–40 seconds): React to [the one specific thing to listen for in this
session — a claim, a demo, a roadmap item]. Say what it means for [the
ambassador's audience: e.g., platform engineers running multi-cluster].
CTA (last 5 seconds): "[Follow the session recording / visit the project booth /
try the project]" + verbal mention of [event hashtag].
Post to: [platform], within 2 hours of the session.
```

## LinkedIn post

Structure: hook line → 2–3 sentences of personal insight (written in the ambassador's professional voice, referencing their real expertise) → what to watch for in the session → tags → hashtags. 80–150 words. No more than 5 hashtags. Tag the event account, the project account, known speaker handles, and speaker companies.

## X / Bluesky post

Under 240 characters. One sharp observation or question, session day/time/room, 2–3 hashtags, tag the speaker if their handle is known. Write a "live" variant (present tense, for posting during the session) and note it as the alternate.

## Hashtag sets

- Base (every post): the official event hashtags (e.g., #KubeCon #CloudNativeCon) — confirm the current official tags from the event site during the run; do not assume.
- Track add-ons: 1–2 per assignment from the session's track (e.g., #PlatformEngineering, #OpenTelemetry, #WASM).
- Never exceed 5 hashtags on LinkedIn or 3 on X/Bluesky.

## Posting recommendation logic

- Own session: one pre-post 2–3 days before ("come see my talk"), one same-day morning reminder, one recap within 24 hours after.
- Keynote assignment: post live or within 1 hour — keynote conversation decays fast.
- Expertise-matched session: recap post within 2 hours works best; video same day.
- Community/booth assignment: any time that day; midday local time gets the most event-floor engagement.
- Pre-event "I'm going" post: 5–7 days before day 1.
- Platform order: LinkedIn first for analysis posts, X/Bluesky first for live coverage.

## Outreach draft template

```
Subject: Your [Event] content assignments are ready

Hi [First name],

Thanks for being a [Project] ambassador — we built you a personalized content
kit for [Event]. You're matched to [N] sessions based on your work in
[expertise]: [one-line list]. Your brief has ready-to-post copy, a short video
prompt for each, and suggested posting times; adjust anything to fit your voice.

First deadline: your pre-event post, [date].

Could you reply to confirm — and if we're missing any of your social handles,
send those too?

[Sender name]
```
