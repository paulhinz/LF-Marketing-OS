# CNCF membership marketing-benefit rules (audit rules engine)

These are the measurable marketing benefits used by the Member Marketing Benefits Audit. Source: CNCF membership benefit descriptions as provided by the CNCF team (Sept 2026). If auditing a later year, re-confirm against the current CNCF membership page before applying.

## Silver
- 2 on-demand Online Programs per year
- 2 vendor-neutral CNCF Blog submissions per year
- Submit announcements to KubeCon + CloudNativeCon news packages (no annual numerical allowance)

## Gold
Includes Silver, plus:
- 4 Online Programs per year — live, on-demand or fireside chat
- 4 vendor-neutral CNCF Blog submissions per year
- Promotion of contributed blogs and Online Programs in CNCF monthly newsletters

## Platinum
Includes Gold, plus:
- Promotion of contributed blogs and Online Programs in the analyst newsletter in addition to the CNCF monthly newsletter
- Exclusive live webinars with CNCF Online Programs

## Academic / Nonprofit
- No stated annual quota for CNCF Blog submissions or Online Programs. Do not invent an allowance.

## End User
- No marketing-benefit quota stated in the provided rules. Mark activity "Activity identified — no annual quota specified" unless the user supplies End User benefit rules.

## Interpretation rules
- Quotas with a number (Blog, Online Programs) are auditable allowances: report Used / Allowance / Remaining.
- Announcements: track usage, report "Activity identified — no annual quota specified."
- Newsletter/analyst promotion and exclusive webinars are qualitative benefits: note observed usage as evidence only; never compute a quota.
- Case Studies, Reports and Whitepapers are not consumed benefits under these rules; audit and count them, but mark "Benefit Counted? = No (not a quota benefit)".
